Delete your account

Farsight

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Farsight and sign in.
  2. Tap the settings icon at the top right of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email farsightappsupport@gmail.com from the address you registered with, using the subject Delete my Farsight account. We will delete your account and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in credentials.
  • Your saved settings (such as your reminder interval).

Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. Reminders already scheduled on your device stop when you delete the account in the app, or when you uninstall it.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.
