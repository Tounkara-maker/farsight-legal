Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Farsight (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by TOUNKARA Mohamed. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run your account.

Information we collect

  • Email address – used to create and secure your account and to send you confirmation and password-reset codes.
  • Password – stored only as a salted, one-way hash by our authentication provider. We cannot read your password.
  • Reminder interval – the number of minutes between break reminders, saved to your account so it can be restored on a new device.
  • Purchase and subscription status – whether you have an active Farsight Plus subscription, plus purchase details such as product, dates and store transaction identifiers. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Sign-in session – a token saved on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Basic technical logs – our provider may record your IP address and request times for security and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect: your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health records, screen content, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Notifications

Break reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s system settings.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To save and restore your reminder interval.
  • To unlock the features included in your subscription.
  • To send account emails (confirmation and password-reset codes).
  • To keep the service secure and to respond to support requests.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) – authentication and database hosting.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) – manages and verifies in-app subscriptions. It receives your account’s random user ID (not your email address), your purchase and subscription information, and basic device and app information such as platform and app version.
  • Apple and Google – process your payment when you subscribe, under their own privacy policies.
  • An email delivery provider – sends confirmation and password-reset emails.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Subscriptions and purchases

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, who charge your store account. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status so the right features are unlocked on your account. Deleting your Farsight account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account), or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your account and associated data from our systems; copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to our database is restricted by access-control rules. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email farsightappsupport@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Farsight is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
